Research software consists of software, code, and tools that allow researchers to generate, analyze, and present data in order to produce research results. Research software engineers (RSEs) are the people who specialize in developing research software. Arizona State University in collaboration with colleagues from Princeton University is exploring the development of a Digital Humanities Research Software Consulting Unit (DHReSCU). This virtual unit will provide digital humanities researchers with the opportunity to consult experienced DH RSEs about their software projects.

Do you have a software project you would like some advice on? Do you have code you'd like to have reviewed? Are you planning a project and would like to talk to someone who understands how to build software? Fill out [this form](https://forms.gle/jff5yk4kyfVACezC8) and we will get back to you\! 

There is no fee for enlisting our help. All we ask for is that you will fill out a short survey afterwards. Please send any questions to dhrescu-contact@googlegroups.com.
